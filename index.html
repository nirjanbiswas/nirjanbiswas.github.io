Nirjan Biswas

NBHM Postdoctoral Researcher · IISER Pune

Thank you for visiting my webpage! I work in the analysis of elliptic partial differential equations, with an emphasis on Hardy–Sobolev inequalities, weighted non‑linear eigenvalue problems, and non‑local operators.

Currently, I am a Post Doctoral Fellow at the Department of Mathematics, IISER Pune. Previously, I held a similar position at TIFR CAM. Prior to that, I worked as a research associate at the Department of Mathematics, IIT Madras under the supervision of Dr. T. V. Anoop. My doctoral degree was awarded on 21 September 2021.

Research Interests

  • Analysis of Elliptic Partial Differential Equations
  • Study of weighted non‑linear eigenvalue problems
  • Hardy–Sobolev inequalities with admissible function spaces
  • Optimization of the first Dirichlet eigenvalue over rearrangement‑invariant classes and under domain perturbations
  • Positivity of solutions on the entire RN for elliptic equations with semipositone nonlinearity
  • Spectrum analysis for double‑phase non‑linear fractional problems
  • Regularity theory for non‑local and mixed local non‑local elliptic equations
  • Bifurcation theory for nonlinear elliptic eigenvalue problems
